🧭 Equations Made Easy

One-step, two-step, distributive, and variables on both sides

📖 Tutorial✏️ Worksheet (20 problems)

1. What does "solve" mean?

An equation is a balance: the left side equals the right side. To solve it, get x alone on one side. Whatever is left on the other side is the answer.

Example: x + 1 = 2. What number plus 1 makes 2? The answer is x = 1.

2. The big idea: move it across, flip its sign

When a number or a variable moves to the other side of the equal sign, it does the opposite.

On this side it is…On the other side it becomes…
+ 5− 5
− 5+ 5
× 3  (like 3x)÷ 3
÷ 4  (like x/4)× 4

Why does this work? Moving is a shortcut for doing the same thing to both sides, which keeps the balance even. Here is x + 1 = 2 both ways:

Do the same to both sides

x + 1 = 2
x + 1 − 1 = 2 − 1
x = 1

+1 and −1 cancel on the left, so x is alone.

Shortcut: move it across

x + 1 = 2
x = 2 − 1 ← +1 jumped over, became −1
x = 1

Same answer, fewer steps.

Careful with multiplying. In 3x = 12, the 3 is multiplied by x. When it moves it becomes ÷ 3, not −3: x = 12 ÷ 3 = 4. And it keeps its own sign: in −2x = 10, x = 10 ÷ (−2) = −5.

3. One-step equations

Only one number is attached to x. Move it across and flip it.

x + 5 = 12
x = 12 − 5
x = 7
x − 3 = 7
x = 7 + 3
x = 10
4x = 20
x = 20 ÷ 4
x = 5
x/3 = 6
x = 6 × 3
x = 18

4. Two-step equations

Two numbers are attached to x. Move the + or − number first, then the × or ÷ number.

3x + 4 = 19
3x = 19 − 4 ← +4 moves, becomes −4
3x = 15
x = 15 ÷ 3 ← ×3 moves, becomes ÷3
x = 5
Why that order? Think of x as a present: it was put in a box (× 3), then wrapped (+ 4). To get it out, take off the wrapping first, then open the box. You undo things in the reverse order they were done.

Check: put 5 back in: 3(5) + 4 = 15 + 4 = 19 ✓

5. Distributive property: why multiply into the parentheses?

3(x + 2) means 3 groups of (x + 2). The 3 multiplies everything inside, not just the x.

x + 2x + 2x + 2 = 3x + 6

Count them: three x's and three 2's. So 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6, not 3x + 2.

Test it with a number. Let x = 4. Then 3(4 + 2) = 3 × 6 = 18. 3x + 6 = 12 + 6 = 18 ✓   3x + 2 = 12 + 2 = 14 ✗

Why we have to distribute: the +2 is locked inside the parentheses with the x. You can't move it across until the parentheses are gone. Distributing opens the parentheses so each piece is free to move.

2(x + 3) = 14
2x + 6 = 14 ← 2·x and 2·3
2x = 14 − 6
2x = 8
x = 8 ÷ 2
x = 4
Watch the minus sign: 4(x − 3) = 4x − 12, because 4 × (−3) = −12.

6. Variables on both sides

Move all the x terms to one side and all the plain numbers to the other. Every term that crosses the equal sign flips its sign.

5x + 2 = 2x + 14
5x − 2x = 14 − 2 ← +2x moved left, +2 moved right
3x = 12
x = 12 ÷ 3
x = 4
Tip: move the smaller x term so the x's stay positive. In 2x + 9 = 5x, move 2x to the right: 9 = 5x − 2x, so 9 = 3x and x = 3.

7. The 4-step plan for any equation

1) Distribute any parentheses.
2) Move x terms to one side and numbers to the other. Flip the sign of anything that crosses.
3) Combine like terms.
4) Divide by the number in front of x. Then check your answer in the original equation.
