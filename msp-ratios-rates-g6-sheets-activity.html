Step 0 · Get the spreadsheet

⬇ Download the spreadsheet
  1. Open Google Drive → New → File upload and choose the file you downloaded.
  2. Double-click it, then choose File → Save as Google Sheets.
  3. Rename it: Your Name – Best Buy Challenge.
Colors: blue numbers are data you may change. Yellow cells are where you type formulas. Every formula starts with =.

Part 1 · Best Buy: Unit Price (tab “1 Best Buy”)

Unit price = price ÷ amount. The smaller unit price is the better buy.

  1. Click F4, type =ROUND(C4/D4,3) and press Enter.
  2. Click F4 again and drag the small blue square in its corner down to F11.
  3. Click G4 and type =IF(F4=MIN(F4:F5),"✓ Best buy",""). Do the same for each pair (rows 6–7, 8–9, 10–11), changing the row numbers.
  4. Look at the chart. The shortest bar in each pair is the best deal.
Think: look at the orange juice. Is the bigger jug always the better buy?

Part 2 · Recipe Scaler: Equivalent Ratios (tab “2 Recipe Scaler”)

The pancake recipe serves 4. The scale factor = number of people ÷ 4.

  1. In C4 type =C3/4.
  2. In D7 type =B7*$C$4 and copy it down. The $ signs lock cell C4 so every row uses the same scale factor.
  3. In E7 type =D7/B7 and copy it down.
  4. Change the number of people in C3 to 8, then 10, then 2. Watch the whole recipe change.

Part 3 · Speed: Miles per Hour (tab “3 Speed”)

Speed = distance ÷ time.

  1. In D4 type =ROUND(B4/C4,1) and copy it down.
  2. Select A3:D8, then Data → Sort range → sort by column D, Z → A (fastest first).

Part 4 · Exit Ticket (tab “4 Exit Ticket”)

Answer the 5 questions in the yellow cells using your results. Then share the sheet with Dr. Park (Share → ppark@starofthesea.org).
