⚖️ Rearranging Formulas

Keep the scale balanced: whatever you do to one side, do to the other.

Big idea: A formula is a balanced scale. To get the variable you want by itself, undo whatever is attached to it, using the inverse operation, on both sides. Multiplying undoes dividing, dividing undoes multiplying, adding undoes subtracting.
