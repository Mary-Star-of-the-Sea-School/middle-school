🧫 Surface Area : Volume Ratio

Why are cells tiny? Grow a cube, cut it up, and watch the ratio change.

Big idea: When a cube's side doubles, its surface area grows 4 times (2²) but its volume grows 8 times (2³). Volume wins, so the SA : V ratio shrinks as things get bigger. Small things have lots of surface for their size.
